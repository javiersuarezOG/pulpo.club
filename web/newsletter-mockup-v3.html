Ocean View Lot Near El Tunco

TAMANIQUE · LA LIBERTAD · 1,263 m²

A twenty-minute walk to one of El Salvador's most famous surf beaches, on a lot with ocean views. The seller inherited it last year and priced it to move — well under what nearby lots are asking. Power runs along the edge of the lot; water you'd bring from the neighbor's well. A small project that ends with you waking up near the surf.

Why we picked it

  • Priced 78% below the area average — rare for a lot this close to the beach
  • 20-minute walk to El Tunco beach, no car needed
  • Power and water already in — you just add the road

What about the price? Lots like this in Tamanique usually go for $200/m². At $47/m², this one is either underpriced or has a catch. The catch we found: water is at the neighbor's, not the lot.

Coffee Farm with Two Rivers

CHILTIUPÁN · LA LIBERTAD · 43 ACRES

$185,000

43 acres total · 9 acres buildable · 2 year-round rivers · 200 balsam trees

If your dream is fog, coffee plants and the sound of a river, look at this one. Forty-three acres in the Balsam mountains, with two year-round rivers and nine acres of coffee already growing. Most of the land is a steep river canyon — you can't build there, but the views from the top ridge are something. The road is rough in rainy season.

Why we picked it

  • Two year-round rivers — water is never the bottleneck
  • 9 buildable acres at the top ridge, with the rest as natural buffer
  • Coffee already growing — income from year one if you keep it

What about the price? $185,000 for 43 acres works out to about $1/m² on paper. The honest number: most of the land is canyon. The 9 buildable acres alone are worth what you're paying.
