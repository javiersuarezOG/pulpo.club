Why the buttons are in the email too

Both buttons are HMAC-signed URLs — they carry their own credential. You can click them from your phone, your laptop, anywhere — no admin login required for that single approval action.

Every recipient gets the same email with the same buttons. First click wins — whoever approves first (you, Sebas, anyone else on the recipient list) fires the audience send. The others see "already approved by ___" if they try after.

The loop

Right now the "Generate test issue" button is greyed out because the code hasn't changed since your last test — generating again would produce the same draft you already rejected.

This is your cue to come to Claude. Tell me what's off ("the welcome lede is too cold", "the coffee farm pick doesn't mention the rivers"), I edit voice_guide.md or the renderer, we merge to main, Vercel deploys.

What gets sent

The audience workflow uses the exact AI-written paragraphs from the test you approved — Pulpo caches them keyed to deploy SHA + listing id so the audience send is reproducible. No surprise variance between what you approved and what your readers receive.

After this fires, the admin returns to step 1 (idle), ready for the next issue.
