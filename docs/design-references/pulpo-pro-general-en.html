What's moving this week.

Surf City 1 = the La Libertad coast (El Tunco → El Sunzal → El Zonte). Surf City 2 = the eastern coast from Las Flores to El Cuco. We cover both.

Two big price cuts this week. A 76,259 m² lot in Chiltiupán (about the size of nine soccer fields — the kind of land a developer buys) and a beachfront home in Tamanique with no work needed both cut their ask. Both are on Surf City 1. The eastern coast (Surf City 2) didn't move this week. If you're looking to buy right now, sellers on the western coast are more open to negotiating.

Weekly News Spotlight

Reported by El Diario de Hoy · 28 May 2026

Why Surf City 2 is the coast's quietest stretch.

The eastern coast (Las Flores → El Cuco) has been our least active stretch this year. The reason is simple: your drive from San Salvador still takes about 2 hours 30 minutes. The government's 2026 road plan includes a new bridge over the Lempa river that's expected to cut your drive to roughly 1h45. If the bridge opens on schedule, you'll be looking at a different market in 12 months. Worth a closer look now while prices are flat — by the time the bridge opens, you may have missed the window.
